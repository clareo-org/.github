[Clareo Health](https://clareohealth.com) turns medical appointments into clear, actionable summaries. You press record at your appointment, and Clareo writes the visit back in plain English: what was decided, what to watch for, every dose and date that was mentioned, and what happens next.

## What Clareo does

- **Appointment summaries.** It records your appointment and turns it into a summary you can actually use, taken from the conversation itself rather than from memory.
- **Answers from your own visits.** Ask a question like "was I meant to stop the other tablet?" and get an answer based only on what was said. If something wasn't said, it tells you so.
- **Plain-English explanations.** Every medical term nobody stopped to define gets explained in plain words.
- **Questions for next time.** It drafts the questions you'd otherwise forget, including the ones that never got a straight answer.
- **Easy sharing.** Send the visit to whoever came with you or whoever couldn't be there, using a link you can revoke at any time.
- **Documents and lab results.** Photograph a letter or a lab printout and it gets read, filed and explained.
- **Clinical trials.** It finds [clinical trials](https://clareohealth.com/clinical-trials) recruiting near you and explains them in plain language.

## Privacy

The audio is deleted as soon as the transcript exists. Only you, and anyone you choose to share a link with, can see a visit. Clareo explains what was said. It never diagnoses, suggests a dose, or tells you to stop a treatment.
